Aluminium sand casting of a scaled steering wheel

2024

Completed a hands‑on manufacturing project involving aluminium sand casting of a scaled steering wheel. Designed a two‑piece 3D‑printed pattern with riser and alignment features, prepared and packed the sand mold, and poured molten aluminium. The final part revealed a gas‑porosity defect, providing insight into venting and casting process optimization.

Design and manufacturing of a Formula Student head restraint

2024

Redesigned and structurally validated the EM‑04 head restraint, a safety‑critical component limiting rearward head motion. Conducted finite element analysis under 890 N central and edge loads to compare three design variants. The final two‑beam configuration with extended supports achieved a Factor of Safety of 2.16 (standard) and 1.35 (worst‑case), meeting all performance criteria and selected for manufacturing.

SolidWorks

Design and manufacturing of a Formula Student pushjack

2024

Redesigned the push‑bar and quick‑jack system for the EM‑04 Formula Student electric vehicle. Developed a combined mechanism that reduced material usage, simplified logistics, and improved operational efficiency during competition. Completed 14 design iterations, achieving a 9.1 kg structure with a safety factor of 1.2, balancing lightweight design and mechanical reliability.

Construction of a gravity powered race car

2018

Collaboratively designed and built a gravity‑powered vehicle for a local downhill race. The chassis was made from wooden boards with a reused seat, combining safety, robustness, and aerodynamic efficiency. Developed a caster‑wheel steering system on reinforced metal brackets for smooth control and a hand‑lever brake applying symmetrical friction on the rear wheels. The project emphasized teamwork, mechanical design, and practical prototyping under real competition conditions.
