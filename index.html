About Me

I'm an engineer focused on automotive development, aerodynamics, and engineering automation, currently at Volkswagen Group Services. I'm driven by the challenge of making complex systems more efficient through Agentic AI, streamlined workflows, and data‑driven decision‑making.

Key achievements

  • Rebuilding a Formula Student aerodynamics department from the ground up, establishing processes, tools and development roadmaps.
  • Improving aerodynamic efficiency (CL/CD) by 260% relative to the EM03 package through concept design and CFD-driven iteration.
  • Achieving 95% time savings in CAD/CAE automation, significantly accelerating engineering cycles.
  • Contributing to a multi‑agent AI system for SEAT body‑in‑white that can give a first manufacturability answer in 30 minutes instead of 4 weeks.

I aim to contribute to high‑performance engineering projects where precision, innovation, and impact come together.

Design of the aerodynamic package of a Formula Student vehicle

2025

Designed the EM05 nosecone, underbody and side wings through 44 CFD iterations, raising CL/CD by 260.59% relative to the EM03 package and moving the centre of pressure from 22% to 41% front.

Altair HyperMesh · AcuSolve

CFD analysis of an LMP1-style prototype

2025

Full-car CFD at 250 km/h with moving ground and rotating wheels, in a team of three. The rear wing carried 798 N of downforce at a lift-to-drag ratio of 7.58, while the front wing's separated upper element made 190 N of lift.

Altair HyperMesh · AcuSolve

Understeer-angle KPI from LMP2 race data

2024

Built an understeer-angle math channel in PI Toolbox from Oreca 07 outing data at Portimão, and an Excel dashboard that tracks the car's balance through a 26-lap stint.

PI Toolbox · Excel

Explicit frontal crash simulation of a vehicle body

2024

Explicit impacts of a half car body at 10 and 20 m/s, in a team of three. Doubling the speed raised peak displacement by 88% and peak plastic strain by 118%, while peak stress stayed near yield.

Altair HyperMesh · Radioss

Aluminium sand casting of a scaled steering wheel

2024

Sand‑cast a scaled steering wheel in aluminium, designing a two‑piece 3D‑printed pattern with risers and alignment features. A gas‑porosity defect in the final part revealed key venting insights.

SolidWorks

Design and manufacturing of a Formula Student pushjack

2024

Combined the EM-04 push-bar and quick-jack into one device. Across 14 logged versions and six concepts, reached a 9.1 kg steel structure with a safety factor of 1.2 at a 300 kg design load.

SolidWorks

Design and manufacturing of a Formula Student head restraint

2024

Redesigned and FE-validated the EM-04 head restraint against the 890 N rule load, comparing three variants. The selected design reached safety factors of 2.16 (centre) and 1.35 (edge, worst case) and passed its scrutineering check at FS Spain 2024.
