Construction of a gravity powered race car

Design and construction of a gravity-powered vehicle for a local downhill competition, carried out collaboratively within a four-person team.

  • Chassis built from wooden boards with a reused seat. Safe, robust, and aerodynamically efficient.

  • Caster wheels on reinforced metal brackets steering system aligned on a perforated steel support bar, creating a mechanism capable of smooth and stable control under downhill loads.

  • The brake uses a hand lever that pivots steel arms to press a rubber pad on the rear wheels, giving a light, reliable, symmetrical friction-based stop.
